1. **Power Crisis Timeline**:
   - By end of 2025: Chip production will exceed ability to power them
   - Current reality: 110,000 GB300s require ~300MW (including cooling, networking)
   - Scaling challenge: Outside China, global electricity output is essentially flat

2. **Space Economics**:
   - Solar panels in space: 5x more effective than Earth (no atmosphere, 24/7 sun)
   - No battery storage needed (continuous sunlight)
   - Cheaper solar cells for space (no weather protection needed)
   - SpaceX targeting 10,000 launches/year (one per hour!)

3. **Manufacturing Revolution - "TeraFab"**:
   - Goal: 100 gigawatts worth of chips by 2030
   - Scale: ~100 million chips running at 1kW each
   - Requires millions of wafers per month
   - Must include logic, memory, AND packaging

1. **SpaceX as Hyperscaler**: Will launch more AI compute annually than Earth's cumulative total within 5 years
2. **xAI's Competitive Edge**: Hardware expertise + ability to scale power = winning combination
3. **Optimus Economics**: Manufacturing 1M units/year with Gen 3, scaling to 10M with Gen 4
